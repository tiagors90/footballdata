About this site

This is a small personal project tracking match results, corners, and cards across seven European football leagues. It started as an Excel spreadsheet and grew into this — a live site reading from a proper database, updated automatically most days.

It's built for one person's curiosity, not as a commercial product — if you've stumbled onto it, feel free to look around, but don't expect guaranteed uptime or accuracy. See the data sources below for exactly where each number comes from.

Where the data comes from
Scores & results
Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, and Liga Portugal are pulled daily from football-data.org's free API.
Updated daily
Corners & cards
For the same six leagues, corners and card counts are backfilled from football-data.co.uk's free downloadable match data.
Updated weekly
Liga 1 (Romania)
Not covered by either automated source above, so every Romanian match — scores, corners, and cards — is entered by hand.
Manual entry
